The Activity Map v1 · seeded from the 2026 hot-companies indexes

An organizational map built on what firms do

Inclusion here is decided by extracted activity — what work a firm performs, which capabilities it operates, which job families it staffs — and never by a registry identifier. Seeded from five 2026 "best companies to work for" indexes, then deliberately expanded past them. Everything is firm-level: no individuals, no contact data.

Built from public firm-level sources (company about / product / careers pages, 10-K Item 1 and segment reporting). Every record carries source URLs and an extraction confidence. Activity clusters are emergent — named after the fact from over-represented capabilities, never assigned from a code list.
